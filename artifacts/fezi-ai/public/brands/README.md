# Brand marks

These marks identify third-party services in FEZI's catalogs and selectors. They are not FEZI trademarks or endorsements.

- SVG marks were downloaded from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) (OpenAI from the Simple Icons v15 archive).
- The Seedance mark is the favicon from ByteDance Seed's official Seedance site. Kling, Wan, Pika, Hailuo, MiniMax, Canva, Adobe, Slack, Runway, LTX, Luma, PixVerse, and Vidu icons were downloaded from the respective official websites.
- For a user-added MCP connection with no known local mark, the client requests only `/favicon.ico` from that connection's public HTTPS origin. It does not send the endpoint path or credentials to a third-party favicon service. Unavailable or unsafe icons have a neutral fallback.

Each trademark remains the property of its owner. Update an asset if its owner changes its branding.
